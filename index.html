Methodology: Kubernetes schedules based on Pod/container resource requests, not actual instantaneous usage. Node Allocatable represents the amount available to Pods. System and kube reservations, eviction reserves, DaemonSet overhead, Pod density and topology/minimum-node constraints are modeled explicitly. Limits are displayed from the source CSV only for audit; they do not drive node count because the scheduler's fit calculation is request-based.

This is a capacity-planning estimator, not a substitute for measuring your actual kubectl describe node allocatable values, DaemonSets, CNI/runtime overhead, ephemeral-storage requirements, topology constraints, taints/tolerations, affinity/anti-affinity and autoscaler behavior in the target cluster.
